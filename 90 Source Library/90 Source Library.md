## How to use the library

1. Follow a source link from a chapter to the relevant section.
2. Treat formulas extracted by OCR cautiously.
3. Distinguish a statement in the article from a practical synthesis in this vault.
4. Do not transport a historical numerical estimate into current markets without new data.

The raw article pages deliberately retain OCR artifacts because they are evidence, not polished reading pages.
