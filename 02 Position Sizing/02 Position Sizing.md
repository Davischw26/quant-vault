# Route 2 · Position Sizing

This route turns “I think I have an edge” into “this is the largest exposure I can justify.”

| Step | Chapter | What it answers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [[Kelly Criterion]] | What size maximizes long-run compound growth? |
| 2 | [[Fractional Kelly and Survival]] | Why should the practical size usually be smaller? |
| 3 | [[From One Bet to a Portfolio]] | How do several positions and preferences change the answer? |

Then use [[Position Sizing Checklist]] for an actual decision.

## Route rule

Kelly is the beginning of the sizing discussion, not the end. The output of the formula is a model-dependent ceiling. Parameter uncertainty, drawdown tolerance, leverage, liquidity, concentration, and funding can only reduce the final size.
