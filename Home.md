## The operating loop

```text
Define the decision
        ↓
Estimate the edge and its uncertainty
        ↓
Choose a size that can survive being wrong
        ↓
Include costs, financing, and liquidity
        ↓
Stress what the model leaves out
        ↓
Test forward, monitor, and stop when evidence changes
```

## What this vault believes

1. **A model is a decision tool, not reality.**
2. **A positive expected return does not tell you how much to risk.**
3. **Full Kelly is an upper bound under unusually strong assumptions.**
4. **Costs and financing are part of the strategy.**
5. **Local risk measures need global stress tests.**
6. **A backtest becomes evidence only after it survives unseen data.**
7. **Staying in the game matters more than maximizing a point estimate.**
