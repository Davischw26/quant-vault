# Research Gap - CEV Exponent and Leverage

## Open questions

Does equity volatility scale as $S^\alpha$ with $0<\alpha<1$, how stable is $\alpha$, and does debt-to-equity explain cross-sectional or time-series variation? The article proposes tests and calls quantitative verification desirable. [[Common Stock Volatilities in Option Formulas#MODELS MAY BE CONSISTENT|Thorp (1976), Black–Scholes and CEV consistency]]; [[Common Stock Volatilities in Option Formulas#VII. TESTING THE COX-ROSS VOLATILITY MODEL|Thorp (1976), CEV test]]

## Research design

Estimate $\alpha$ by the paper’s normalized-increment regression and by likelihood methods; stratify by leverage; compare option pricing and hedge errors with the geometric-Brownian baseline; and use forward periods to avoid selecting the best exponent in sample.
